# PDFeditz product record

## Platform

web

## Users

People who need to complete PDF tasks quickly in a browser, including office workers, students, freelancers, and privacy-conscious users handling sensitive documents.

## Purpose

PDFeditz provides browser-based PDF organization, editing, conversion, security, OCR, and document utility workflows without requiring an account.

## Positioning

A free, local-first PDF workspace that keeps common processing in the browser and remains suitable for static hosting.

## Constraints

- No mandatory account, database, paid API, analytics, or cloud storage.
- Preserve client-side processing wherever technically feasible.
- Keep static deployment and localization support.
- Preserve accessibility and required legal notices.

## Brand

The product is named PDFeditz and is developed under AkshHQ. Use a text wordmark until a logo is supplied.
